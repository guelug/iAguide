## Tokens are the unit of the bill

Rough head arithmetic: cost ≈ (input tokens × input price) + (output tokens × output price), plus tool APIs, plus the machines you leave on. **Prefix cache** discounts the stable front of the prompt when the vendor supports it — you still pay for new tokens. Local is “free” until you amortise the box, power, and your time.

Break-even is a spreadsheet, not a tweet. Measure your real mix of input/output and cache hit rate before you swear loyalty to an API or a GPU.

## Case — CacheMiss Co.

CacheMiss Co. paid full input rate because a timestamp in the system prompt changed every request. They “optimised” the model. Removing the timestamp restored cache hits and halved the bill. The weights were innocent.
