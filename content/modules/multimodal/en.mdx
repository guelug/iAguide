## The residual stream does not care what you called the token

Vision, speech, and video models still predict the next unit. A vision encoder turns patches of an image into vectors that land in the same residual stream as text tokens. Diffusion and flow matching generate pixels by iterating a noise process — different clothes, same “take a step given the state” habit.

For **agents**, the interesting case is not “caption this photo.” It is: the observation *is* an image (a screenshot, a document page, a game frame), and the action may be “click,” “crop,” or “look again.”

## Vision agents and browser agents

Hugging Face’s smolagents can pass images at the start of a run, or capture screenshots during a web loop and stash them on the action step. A browser agent that cannot *forget* old screenshots will fill the window with pixels and then loop.

The particularity of that library lives in [smolagents: retrieval, teams, vision](/en/m/smolagents-multi). The general rule lives here: **an image is an observation, not a personality.**

## Case — AtlasBrowse’s endless screenshot

AtlasBrowse gave a vision agent `max_steps=20` and a callback that saved every Selenium screenshot onto the log. By step 8 the prompt was four nearly identical images of a cookie banner the agent could not dismiss. It kept “looking.” They added `close_popups`. They should also have dropped images older than two steps.
