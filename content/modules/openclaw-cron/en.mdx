## Scheduler inside the Gateway

openclaw automations (alias cron). Mutations need operator.admin. Agent jobs default isolated (fresh transcript).

Delivery: announce, webhook, or none. Isolated jobs can skip bootstrap with --light-context. Jobs live in shared SQLite.

Hermes: jobs.json plus a fresh AIAgent. OpenClaw: Gateway-owned SQLite with isolated or main session choice.
