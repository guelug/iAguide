## One GPU is never enough (for pretrain)

To train large models you **split work**:

- **Data parallel** — copies of the model, different batches, gradient sync.
- **Tensor parallel** — split a layer’s matmuls across devices.
- **Pipeline parallel** — split layers into stages; microbatches keep the pipe full.
- **Expert parallel** — MoE experts live on different devices.
- **ZeRO / FSDP** — shard optimizer state (and often params) so memory fits.

The interconnect (NVLink, InfiniBand) becomes the wall. A laptop can LoRA. It cannot honestly pretrain a frontier base.

## Case — Startup’s “8× GPU” with PCIe

A startup striped an 8× consumer GPU box over PCIe and expected tensor-parallel 70B training. Gradient sync ate the week. They needed a machine with a fabric, or a smaller model. The parallelism diagram was right; the bus was not.
