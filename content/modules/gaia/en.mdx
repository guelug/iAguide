## What GAIA measures

[GAIA](https://huggingface.co/papers/2311.12983) is a benchmark of **466 questions** that are conceptually easy for people (~92%) and hard for assistants (early GPT-4 + plugins ~15%). Tasks need multi-step reasoning, tools, sometimes multimodal hops. Levels 1–3 add steps and tool coordination. Answers are short and factual so scoring is simple — and so brute force fails.

A public leaderboard on Hugging Face hosts testing questions. That is useful. **It is not your product.** iAguide does not host their certificate and will not pretend a quiz in this course equals GAIA.

Unit 4 of the Agents Course is “build an agent, submit to the leaderboard, maybe earn a certificate.” Steal the *shape*: pick hard, tool-using questions that match *your* domain. Skip Discord onboarding and live Q&A.

What to learn next, in their words: MCP (tools as a socket) and A2A (agents talking to agents). We fold MCP into [tools](/course/tools-and-mcp); multi-agent into [patterns](/course/agent-patterns) and [smolagents-multi](/course/smolagents-multi).

## Case — Quizly’s 9/10 and a silent GAIA

Quizly trained support staff on a twenty-question multiple choice about their agent. Average 9/10. The same agent scored near zero on three internal GAIA-style tasks (need a PDF + a calendar + a calculator). The quiz measured vocabulary. GAIA measured the loop. They shipped on the quiz.
