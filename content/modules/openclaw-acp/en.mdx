## Drive other harnesses, or be one

ACP via @openclaw/acpx spawns Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini CLI, and others. Native Codex stays on /codex; ACP is explicit.

/acp spawn --bind here pins the current chat. --thread auto may create a child thread. Gateway commands stay local.

ACP runs on the host, not inside OpenClaw sandbox. Sandboxed sessions cannot spawn ACP. openclaw acp is the inverse: expose a Gateway session as an ACP server.

## Compare vs Hermes

Hermes ACP adapter wraps AIAgent as the server. OpenClaw more often spawns an external harness, and can also serve ACP.

## Case: spawn from a group

**Case.** Sandbox non-main blocks sessions_spawn runtime acp.
