## Freshness is a policy

Default mode none: same sessionId forever; compaction handles growth. daily: new session at session.reset.atHour (default 4 local) based on sessionStartedAt. idle: new session after idleMinutes based on lastInteractionAt — heartbeat/cron/exec do NOT keep it alive.

Whichever expires first wins. resetByType and resetByChannel override. /new or /reset is the manual path.

## Compare vs Hermes

Hermes compaction is in-place or lineage (parent_session_id); no daily/idle session roll by default. OpenClaw can roll the id on a clock without compacting.
