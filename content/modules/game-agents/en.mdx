## Particularity — from chatty NPCs to legal actions

An LLM NPC can talk in character. An **agent** in a game chooses an action the engine will accept. Turn-based games (Pokémon-style combat) leave enough wall-clock for a thought-action-observation loop. Twitch plays of Claude in Pokémon show the other side: real-time FPS budgets do not wait for a 2k-token plan.

Hugging Face Bonus Unit 3 wires this with **poke-env** (Python `Player` API), **Pokémon Showdown**, an `LLMAgentBase` that formats battle state and exposes `choose_move` / `choose_switch`, and a `TemplateAgent` you fill with your provider. The observation is the battle string; the action must match available moves or switches; illegal plans fall back to random.

## Case — RivalAI’s forbidden Hydro Pump

RivalAI’s agent planned Hydro Pump because the lore said Water beats Fire. The active Pokémon had not learned it — PP list was empty for that id. The base class fell back to random and the stream laughed. They prompted “only use available moves.” They also needed `_find_move_by_name` to fail closed, which it did; the case is the prompt’s optimism versus the state’s law.
