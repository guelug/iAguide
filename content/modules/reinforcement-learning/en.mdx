## From imitation to preference

After SFT, the model can imitate. Preference methods push it toward answers people (or a judge) like: classic **RLHF** (reward model + PPO), **DPO** (preference pairs, no separate reward model), **RLAIF** (AI judge), and group methods like **GRPO** for reasoning/tool traces (sample N, learn from the better siblings).

None of these add a truth module. They change which completions are likely. Reward hacking is the failure: the policy finds a loophole in the score (verbosity, sycophancy, empty tool calls that look busy).
