## The part that decides quality

Weights imitate what they see. A beautiful trainer on a dirty mix produces a confident mess. **Sourcing** (where text came from), **dedup** (near-duplicates inflate epochs), **filtering** (PII, spam, empty shells), **contamination** (eval leaked into train), **synthetic** data (useful, biased like the teacher), and **licences** (can you ship this) are the job. Not glamorous. Decisive.

Pretrain corpora are industrial. For SFT and tool traces you can own the set: a few hundred clean pairs in the template you will serve beat a scraped dump.

## Case — EchoLab’s duplicated FAQ

EchoLab fine-tuned on a support export. One FAQ was pasted 400 times. Loss looked great. The model answered that FAQ and shrugged everything else. Dedup would have shown a 12-file dataset, not a 5_000-row fantasy.
